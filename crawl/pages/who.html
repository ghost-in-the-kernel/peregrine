SBA (8a) Certified Leading AI, Data Science, and Strategy Firm

We help stand-up, operate, and optimize technology and data oriented government agencies and offices

Peregrine Advisors Benefit Inc. is one of the select SBA 8(a) Certified Small Business Benefit Corporations specializing in data and technology advisory services for government agencies. We combine deep technical expertise with innovative strategic thinking to transform how government agencies leverage their data and technology assets, particularly supporting Offices of the Chief Data Officers (OCDOs), Chief Artificial Intelligence (AI) Officers, Chief Technology Officers, Chief Statistical Officers, Chief Innovation Officers, and Chief Analytics Officers.

We solve our clients’ most pressing data problems

Our bespoke enterprise-level solutions span data science, engineering, strategy, analytics, governance, management, architecture, and security. We create optimal data environments that promote robust data cultures through disciplined data use. Using our unique and proven approach, our team of data scientists and specialists collaborate closely with client subject matter experts and key stakeholders to convert ever-growing and increasingly complex data from an organizational challenge into an enterprise asset.

We prepare our clients for the future

Known for our creativity, resilience, and agility, our team of highly-trained strategists and data scientists brings a future-oriented perspective to our clients' AI and data initiatives. We stay ahead of the curve by keenly tracking industry developments and maintaining close connections with cutting-edge academic research. This enables us to imagine, design, architect, implement, and operate innovative solutions that solve our clients' most intractable problems.

With a practice-based culture centered on extreme commitment to client value creation, we bring creativity, tenacity, and agility to solve complex problems in the most challenging environments. Our holistic enterprise approach balances technology with human-centered design, ensuring our clients achieve their mission goals through cloud, Big Data, and AI/ML initiatives, enhanced by design-based enterprise-level training programs that consistently deliver outstanding outcomes."
