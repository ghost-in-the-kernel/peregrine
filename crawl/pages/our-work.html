Our Work

  • Peregrine Advisors partnered with the SEC's Office of the Chief Data Officer to develop and implement their comprehensive Data Strategy for FY 2022-2026, establishing a framework that transforms how the agency leverages data as a strategic asset. Our team of data strategists and architects helped design the roadmap to enhance data accessibility, build integrated infrastructure, promote data literacy, and strengthen governance—enabling the SEC to better protect investors and maintain fair, orderly markets through data-driven decision making.

  • Peregrine Advisors transformed the SEC's complex regulatory data into accessible, interactive visualizations that enhance public transparency and empower investors with intuitive insights on market trends and regulatory information. Our team of data visualization experts designed and implemented cutting-edge dashboards that showcase SEC data in a mobile-responsive, user-friendly format, supporting the SEC's mission to promote fair and efficient markets while helping stakeholders make more informed decisions through compelling visual narratives. See public visualizations here: SEC Data Visualizations.

  • At Peregrine Advisors, we recognize that cryptocurrency markets represent a significant evolution in financial systems and data analytics. As blockchain technology and digital assets continue to mature, financial institutions and regulatory bodies increasingly need sophisticated tools to monitor, analyze, and understand these dynamic markets.

    Our team developed this interactive cryptocurrency dashboard to demonstrate how complex financial data can be transformed into actionable insights through intuitive visualization. This tool represents our approach to making sophisticated technical analysis accessible.

    Interactive Analysis Tools

    Our cryptocurrency dashboard provides real-time technical analysis with multiple industry-standard indicators:

    • Moving Averages (SMA & EMA) for trend identification

    • Relative Strength Index (RSI) for momentum analysis

    • Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) for signal detection

    • Bollinger Bands for volatility assessment

    Users can select from popular cryptocurrencies and adjust timeframes from daily to yearly views, enabling comprehensive market analysis tailored to specific needs.
